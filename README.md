## What I'm building

**[Stream AI Workout Assistant](https://github.com/seth-cohen18/stream-ai)** is a web app I founded and build myself. Prop up a phone or webcam, do your set, and it counts your reps and coaches your form live. No wearables, no trainer.

- Pose detection with MediaPipe, plus rule-based form checks on 2D landmarks for 11 lifts
- A 1,400+ exercise library, a workout builder, guided yoga, and GPS run tracking
- Nutrition tracking with barcode scan, photo meal logging, and nutrition-label scan
- **PUMP**, a Claude-powered in-app coach with safety guardrails
- Flask and Firestore on Google Cloud, with 7,000+ automated tests and 100+ live-site health checks in CI

**Something I learned the hard way:** my first form detector was an LSTM trained on synthetic data. It scored well on test data and got about 20% on real video. I scrapped it and moved to geometric rules on pose landmarks, which I can test and debug one exercise at a time.
